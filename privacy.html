Privacy policy

Worklist · effective 21 November 2026

Worklist is a personal to-do list used only by its developer. This policy describes what it does with Google user data.

What it accesses

  • Your calendar list (read-only): the names of your calendars, to choose which to show.
  • Your calendar events (read-only): titles, times and places, to show appointments beside tasks.
  • Calendars Worklist creates: it creates, updates and deletes events only on its own calendar.

How the data is used and kept

Calendar data is used only to show and maintain the owner's own to-do list. It is stored only on the owner's own computers. Sign-in tokens are kept on those computers and are never sent anywhere but Google.

Sharing

Worklist does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. It uses no advertising, no analytics and no third-party servers. Nobody but the owner can see the data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Worklist's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page. Deleting Worklist's local files removes everything it stored.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
