Worklist

A personal to-do list that runs on its owner's own computers.

Worklist keeps one person's running list of tasks, chores and appointments. It is a private tool for its developer's own use, not a public service: there is nothing to sign up for or download here.

How it uses Google Calendar

With the owner's permission, Worklist connects to their Google Calendar to:

  • read the list of their calendars and the events on them, so appointments show beside tasks;
  • create and update events on a calendar that Worklist itself created, so dated tasks reach their phone.

It cannot change or delete events on any other calendar.
